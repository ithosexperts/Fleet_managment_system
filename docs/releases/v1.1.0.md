### 🚀 What's New in v1.1.0:

#### 1. Multi-Select Checkbox Dropdown Filters
- **Interactive Checkbox Selectors:** Filter operations schedules, fleet documents, and dispatch runs by multiple statuses, drivers, or vehicle types simultaneously.
- **Selection Count Badges:** Active selection counts displayed directly in pill badges with 1-click clear-all actions.
- **Smart Edge Auto-Alignment:** Viewport boundary detection automatically flips dropdown menus leftward near screen edges to eliminate mobile and tablet text clipping.

#### 2. Driver In-Cab Ergonomics & Cockpit Redesign
- **1-Tap Vehicle Papers Action:** Instant access to all statutory compliance certificates (Registration Certificate, Fitness, Insurance, Pollution Under Control, National Permit) directly from the active trip card.
- **Streamlined Cab Navigation:** Removed SOS tab from primary navigation headers to maintain focused operational workflows.
- **Enriched Trip Telemetry:** Manifest Trip ID pill badges, live distance in kilometers, and estimated time of arrival (ETA) on upcoming delivery destinations.
- **Mobile Gesture Ergonomics:** Bottom safe-area inset padding ensuring uninterrupted mobile thumb navigation and gestures.

#### 3. Operational Governance & Delay Attribution Analytics
- **Dual-Series Delay Line Chart:** Visualized tracking distinguishing delays caused by operational management (route reassignment, loading delays, dock congestion) versus driver transit delays.
- **Delivery Deletion Protection:** Locked delivery action states protecting in-transit and completed orders against accidental data loss, accompanied by compliance tooltips.
- **Compliance Photo & Document Uploads:** Direct upload support for driver profile photos and vehicle statutory papers with instant synchronization.

#### 4. Cross-Device Responsive Layouts
- Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile CSS grid adaptations.
- Horizontal scrollable filter bars on mobile devices with sticky actions and seamless touch scrolling.
